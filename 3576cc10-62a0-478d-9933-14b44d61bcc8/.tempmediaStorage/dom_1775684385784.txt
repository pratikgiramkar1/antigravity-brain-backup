	[1] (500,533)<summary >How does Prism handle PCI compliance? />
	Prism is PCI-Compliant by design. It operates as a stateless library with no data storage, no logging - meaning no PCI data is ever persisted. Prism provides you with the flexibility to outsource your PCI compliance to third-party vaults/ payment processors of your choice, or insource the compliance fully.

	[3] (500,613)<summary >Who develops and maintains the Prism library ? />
	Prism is actively maintained by the Juspay Hyperswitch team with regular updates for new additions, features, security patches, and bug fixes. It is an actively used component on the Juspay hyperswitch platform powering payments for large enterprises.

	[5] (500,692)<summary >How many payment processors and methods does Prism support? />
	Prism supports 60+ connectors including Stripe, Adyen, Braintree, PayPal, and Worldpay. It covers 100+ payment method types across multiple categories: Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.), Digital Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal), Online Banking (iDEAL, Sofort), Bank Transfers (SEPA, ACH), Buy Now Pay Later (Klarna, Affirm), Cryptocurrency, and Regional methods like UPI, PIX and Boleto.
[6] (500,803)<details class='faq-item'>No data is logged to any server. The library is credential-free, and can be embedded into your app without any risks or compliance involved. Just like an optical prism, it takes an input (beam of light), transforms (refracts) and sends it to the target payment processor endpoint of your choice. />
	[7] (500,783)<summary >How is sensitive data protected when using Prism? />
	No data is logged to any server. The library is credential-free, and can be embedded into your app without any risks or compliance involved. Just like an optical prism, it takes an input (beam of light), transforms (refracts) and sends it to the target payment processor endpoint of your choice.
